ManagerTalk

Practise difficult management conversations.

Most management training tells you what good feedback looks like. ManagerTalk makes you do it: work through realistic workplace situations, write what you would actually say, and get scored, specific feedback.

Coming soon to Google Play

How it works

  • Start with the evidence: what was said before and what is at stake for each person.
  • Work the conversation stage by stage, in your own words — not multiple choice.
  • Get scored on behaviour, with what worked, what did not, and the one thing to change next time.
  • Finish with a certificate of completion.

Who it is for

New and experienced managers, team leads, and anyone with a conversation they have been putting off.
