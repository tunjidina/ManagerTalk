ManagerTalk

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026 · Last updated 24 September 2026

ManagerTalk is a scenario-based manager-training app. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible for your data

ManagerTalk is operated by Adetunji Odedina, based in the United Kingdom, acting as the data controller for the purposes of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

For any privacy question or request, contact tunjidina12@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo create and sign you in to your account, and to send password-reset emails.
PasswordStored only as a salted hash by Google Firebase Authentication. The app never sees or stores your password.
Display name (optional)Populated if your sign-in provider supplies one.
Certificate name (optional)The name you choose to appear on a completion certificate. You enter this yourself and may leave it blank.
Scenario progressWhich scenario you are on and whether you have completed it, so you can resume where you left off. The text of your responses stays on your device and is not sent to us.
Progress eventsTimestamped records of scenario steps completed, used to show your own progress and to understand which scenarios are finished or abandoned.
Purchase recordsWhether you hold the premium entitlement, and the associated transaction, so your purchase works across devices.
Account timestampsWhen your account was created and last used.

What we do not collect

  • Payment card details. Card data is entered into a checkout page operated by Stripe and is never transmitted to, processed by, or stored by ManagerTalk.
  • Location data.
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files on your device.
  • Advertising identifiers. ManagerTalk shows no advertising and contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

Legal basis for processing

  • Performance of a contract — creating your account, saving your progress, and delivering a purchase you have made.
  • Legitimate interests — understanding in aggregate which training scenarios are completed or abandoned, so the app can be improved. You may object to this at any time using the contact address above.
  • Legal obligation — retaining transaction records where tax or accounting law requires it.

Who processes data on our behalf

ManagerTalk does not sell personal data and does not share it for advertising. The following processors handle data in order to run the service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — account credentials, profile and progress data.
  • RevenueCat — purchase and entitlement records, keyed to your account identifier.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe acts as an independent controller for payment data under its own privacy policy.

These providers may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or an applicable adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

Account, profile and progress data is retained for as long as your account exists. Transaction records are retained for six years, as UK tax law requires. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except records we are legally required to keep.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email tunjidina12@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. To delete your account, see below.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Delete your ManagerTalk account

You can delete your ManagerTalk account and its data at any time, in either of two ways.

  • In the app: go to Scenarios, open Profile, and choose Delete account. You will be asked for your password to confirm.
  • By email: send a request from the address you signed up with to tunjidina12@gmail.com with the subject “ManagerTalk account deletion”.

What is deleted: your sign-in account, email address, certificate name and scenario progress. In-app deletion removes these immediately. Progress events, and everything for email requests, are removed within 30 days.

What is kept: purchase transaction records, for six years, as UK tax law requires.

Children

ManagerTalk is a workplace training tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to your stored data is restricted by server-side security rules so that each account can read and write only its own records. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and, where the change affects how your data is used, we will notify you by email.
